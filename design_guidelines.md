# Design Guidelines for Mobile Tyre Van Website

## Design Approach
**Selected Approach**: Reference-Based with Industrial/Automotive Focus
Drawing inspiration from automotive and industrial service providers like Ford Commercial, Mercedes-Benz Vans, and conversion specialists, emphasizing trust, capability, and professional service delivery.

## Core Design Elements

### A. Color Palette
**Primary Colors:**
- Primary: #111827 (dark slate) - Deep 220 15% 11%
- Accent Yellow: #FACC15 - Energetic 48 95% 53%
- Accent Blue: #2563EB - Professional 217 91% 60%

**Supporting Colors:**
- Background: Pure white for maximum contrast
- Text: #374151 for secondary text
- Borders: #E5E7EB for subtle divisions

### B. Typography
**Font System**: Inter via Google Fonts CDN
- Headlines: Inter Bold (font-bold) - sizes from text-4xl to text-6xl
- Subheadings: Inter SemiBold (font-semibold) - text-xl to text-2xl
- Body: Inter Regular (font-normal) - text-base to text-lg
- Captions: Inter Medium (font-medium) - text-sm

### C. Layout System
**Spacing Framework**: Tailwind units of 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24
- Standard padding: p-6, p-8
- Section spacing: mb-12, mb-16, mb-24
- Component margins: m-4, m-6, m-8
- Grid gaps: gap-6, gap-8

### D. Component Library

**Core UI Elements:**
- Cards: rounded-2xl with soft shadows (shadow-lg)
- Buttons: rounded-xl with bold typography, primary yellow (#FACC15) and blue (#2563EB) variants
- Form inputs: rounded-lg borders with focus states in accent colors
- Navigation: sticky header with prominent CTA button

**Industrial Design Features:**
- Bold, chunky elements suggesting durability
- High contrast for outdoor/mobile viewing
- Clear visual hierarchy emphasizing trust and capability
- Generous whitespace for professional appearance

**Data Displays:**
- Spec tables with alternating row colors
- Price badges with prominent VAT indicators
- Filter panels with checkbox and range inputs
- Stock cards featuring large vehicle images

**Trust Elements:**
- Badge components for certifications (FCA, nationwide delivery)
- Testimonial marquees with customer logos
- Progress indicators for multi-step configurator
- Security badges and trust signals

### E. Visual Treatment

**Photography Style:**
- High-quality vehicle photography with clean backgrounds
- Before/after conversion galleries
- Professional facility and team photos
- Mobile-optimized image delivery with srcset

**Interactive Elements:**
- Configurator stepper with clear progress indication
- Real-time price calculations with smooth updates
- Lightbox galleries for vehicle details
- Smooth transitions between configurator steps

**Layout Patterns:**
- Hero sections with strong calls-to-action
- Grid-based stock listings with consistent card heights
- Two-column layouts for detailed specifications
- Mobile-first responsive design throughout

## Images
