CH-E · Questions

Asked before you ask.

Which industries does Chassis work for?

Any business where a customer configures a vehicle from choices — base vehicle, equipment package, upgrades. Tyre-fitting vans, camper conversions and horsebox builds ship as ready-made verticals today; catering trucks, welfare vans, dog-grooming vans and mobile workshops are config away, not code away.

Will my site look like other Chassis sites?

No — that's the point. Each deployment gets its own theme: palette, fonts, shape language, imagery and copy voice. Two Chassis sites in different industries share nothing visually. Only the admin panel is deliberately consistent, so support and training stay excellent.

Do I need my own developers?

No. Chassis is delivered as a managed platform: we deploy, host, maintain and update it. You manage vehicles, pricing, content and your pipeline through the admin panel.

What about my accountant?

Keep them. Chassis pushes invoices to Sage, Xero or QuickBooks with correct line items and VAT treatment — your accountant sees clean books in the software they already use.

Who owns my data?

You do. Your customers, quotes and build history live in your deployment's own database, exportable on request. Leaving is unpleasant for us, not impossible for you.
